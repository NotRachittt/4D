## 📌 About

This is the source code for my personal thesis website on the **fourth dimension** in physics.

Built as a 2nd year undergraduate project, the site covers how our understanding of dimensions evolved from classical Newtonian space all the way to the open frontiers of quantum gravity.

---

## 🧭 What's Covered

- Classical 3D space and why it wasn't enough
- Special relativity and Minkowski spacetime
- General relativity — gravity as the curvature of 4D spacetime
- Quantum mechanics and its uneasy relationship with spacetime
- Open questions — unification, extra dimensions, the holographic principle
